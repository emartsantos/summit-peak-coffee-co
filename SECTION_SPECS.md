# PHASE 4 - Section Specifications

## 1. SIGNATURE HERO
**Goal**: Memorable first impression, establish brand

- **Headline**: "Summit Peak Coffee Co." or refined variant - "Crafted at altitude. Brewed with intention." or "The summit of specialty coffee"
- **Subheadline**: Concise positioning - origin transparency, craftsmanship
- **Primary CTA**: "Explore Our Coffee" / "Taste the Craft" (links to offerings/contact)
- **Navigation**: Minimal, premium - logo + nav links + mobile menu
- **Visual**: Art-directed hero image (roastery, beans, cup) - editorial treatment
- **Animation**: Controlled text reveal on load (CSS-based), subtle
- **Mobile**: Stacked, generous padding, readable type

## 2. BRAND INTRODUCTION
**Goal**: Editorial storytelling, differentiate

- **Layout**: Split or asymmetrical - text + image
- **Copy**: Concise - "Specialty coffee roaster & café for those who value origin, craft, and intention"
- **Typography**: Focus on refined hierarchy, generous line-height
- **Visual**: Atmospheric imagery showing craft/space
- **Approach**: Editorial, not marketing fluff

## 3. FEATURED OFFERINGS
**Goal**: Showcase products/services without generic card grid overdose

- **Items**: Coffee beans (single origin, blends), house drinks, retail
- **Layout**: Custom - may be magazine-style, not uniform cards
- **Hierarchy**: Strong visual focus per item
- **CTAs**: Link to inquiry/contact for each
- **Image Treatment**: Editorial product photography

## 4. BRAND DIFFERENTIATORS
**Goal**: Evidence-based advantages

- **Points**: Origin transparency, careful sourcing, small-batch roasting, craftsmanship, quality
- **Presentation**: Elegant, typography-led or with minimal icons
- **Avoid**: Generic icons for every point; be selective
- **Trust**: Subtle signals, no fake stats

## 5. VISUAL SHOWCASE
**Goal**: Art-directed gallery

- **Composition**: Mix of scales, controlled asymmetry, breathing room
- **Images**: Café interior, roasting, beans, latte art, community (authentic)
- **Responsive**: Intelligently reflow, not just grid collapse
- **Treatment**: Next/Image, optimized

## 6. PROCESS / EXPERIENCE
**Goal**: Explain customer journey

- **Steps**: Sourcing → Roasting → Brewing/Service
- **Narrative**: Story-driven, editorial
- **Layout**: Varied per step, engaging without carousel overload

## 7. TRUST / SOCIAL PROOF
**Goal**: Only real, verified content

- **Priority**: No invented testimonials. If none provided, adapt - focus on credibility (process, values)
- **Options**: Brand story, credentials, press if real, IG reference
- **Constraint**: "Never invent ratings or customer reviews"

## 8. CONVERSION / CONTACT
**Goal**: Primary inquiry conversion

- **Headline**: Clear, refined CTA
- **Form**: Inquiry form - name, email, message, subject (what they want to know)
- **States**: Loading, success, error
- **Contact**: Email, phone, WhatsApp (approved), hours, location
- **Footer**: Minimal, refined - logo, nav, social links (IG), copyright
